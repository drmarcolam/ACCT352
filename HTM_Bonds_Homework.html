Held-to-Maturity Bond Investments Practice

Recording a discounted bond investment at effective yield, applying the effective-interest method to amortize the discount, and reporting the investment on the balance sheet and statement of cash flows.

Rounding convention: round every dollar figure (interest revenue, discount amortization, carrying amounts, journal entry amounts) to the nearest whole dollar.

Problem 1 of 1

Fuzzy Technologies, Incorporated

Fuzzy Technologies, Incorporated purchased as a long-term investment $100 million of 8% bonds, dated January 1, on January 1, 2027. Management has the positive intent and ability to hold the bonds until maturity.

For bonds of similar risk and maturity the market yield was 10%. The price paid for the bonds was $82 million. Interest is received semiannually on June 30 and December 31.

Due to changing market conditions, the fair value of the bonds at December 31, 2027, was $90 million.
